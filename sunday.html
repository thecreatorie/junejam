Day Three

Sunday
· Dark ·

The forest closes the ritual.

Shadow·Shimmer·Rite

Day three closes the festival in shadow and shimmer. The night belongs to Astralys, who descends only after dusk. Aerial moves into ritual geometry. Flow opens with Prisma's prismatic disco-ball moment, then weaves into Zahara's slow serpent dance under low light.

Stiltwalking · Roaming · Night Only

Astralys

Keeper of the Quiet Sky — Ethereal Star Goddess

She descends only after dusk. Her presence hushes a crowd. She doesn't glow so much as gleam, the way moonlight gleams on still water.

Costume
Flowing whites, silvers, and antique golds; hand-set crystals and metallic threadwork; LED wings that unfurl like a slow-breathing galaxy; constellation crown
Makeup
Pearlescent base, silver-leaf accents along the temples, soft champagne shimmer, ivory lip
Energy
Long, suspended, oceanic — as if gravity is something she's only agreed to honor temporarily
Mood Board
Greek goddess statuary · Galadriel · NASA Hubble imagery · baroque pearl jewelry · moonlit water
Palette

Tone — Celestial, reverent, slow-time

Aerial · Apparatus: Lyra or Pentagram

Thornwyn

the Goth Fae — Dark Fae

A fae who took the long road through shadow. She moves like ink in water — fluid, twisting, melancholy-beautiful. The one who waits in the grove after sundown.

Costume
Sheer black bodysuit hand-painted with swirling vine and smoke patterns, sharp little horns or twisted antler crown
Makeup
Kohl-smudged eyes, lips dark as wine, paled-out base, smoky under-eye shadow
Apparatus
Lyra or pentagram
Energy
Moody, sensual, hauntingly elegant. Audiences feel something private when she performs.
Mood Board
Pre-Raphaelite paintings · gothic fairy tales · ink-in-water photography · Tim Burton's gentler work
Palette

Tone — Moody, sensual, hauntingly elegant

Aerial · Apparatus: Pentagram

Morwenna

the Five-Point Rite — Pentagram

Occult ceremonial energy. Her routine is structured around the geometry of the five points — shapes that map a star in the air.

Costume
Black and silver costume, sigil details across the bodysuit, ornate ritual headpiece
Makeup
Ritual makeup — symbolic markings, dark eye, silver-flecked highlight, witchy contour
Apparatus
Pentagram
Energy
Ritual, mystical, theatrical. Slow incense-smoke movement quality. Mysterious, slightly witchy.
Mood Board
Sacred geometry · ceremonial magic · The Witch (2015) · tarot illustrations · alchemical diagrams
Palette

Tone — Ritual, mystical, theatrical

Prisma

the Disco Oracle — Diamond / Rhinestone

A reflective, prismatic figure who turns every spotlight into a thousand spotlights — her quad rhinestone hoops throw shards of light across the entire crowd like a living disco ball.

Costume
Mirror-tile bodysuit, crystal headpiece, silver-glittered skin, rhinestone-encrusted everything
Makeup
Rhinestone face accents, glitter highlight, crystal clusters at the temple, silver-foil eye
Prop
Quad rhinestone hoops (stage-friendly, light-reflective)
Energy
Pure stage moment. Showgirl-meets-light-priestess: big arm extensions, slow turns to maximize light play, stillness when the beat hits.
Mood Board
Disco balls · diamond cuts · Studio 54 glamour · mirror mosaics · Bob Mackie costumes
Palette

Tone — Glamorous, hypnotic, main-stage spectacle

Flow Arts · Prop: LED Dragon Staff

Zahara

the Serpent's Daughter — LED Dragon Staff

Belly dance meets dragon spirit on the main stage. The LED staff coils around her body like a living creature — isolations, undulations, slow staff manipulations weaving the dragon between her arms and hips.

Costume
Coined hip scarf, ornate bra and skirt set in jewel tones, gold body chains, dragon-scale or serpentine motifs
Makeup
Kohl-lined eyes, jewel-toned eye shadow (deep teal or aubergine), gold cheekbone shimmer, deep berry lip
Prop
LED dragon staff (programmable patterns)
Energy
Ancient, hypnotic, sinuous. Tribal fusion belly dance precision meets staff fluidity.
Mood Board
Tribal fusion belly dance · Chinese dragon dances · snake-charmer iconography · art nouveau serpent jewelry
Palette

Tone — Ancient, hypnotic, sinuous
