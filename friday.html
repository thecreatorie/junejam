Day One

Friday
· Neon ·

The forest wakes electric.

UV·LED·Glow

Bright. Fast. Otherworldly. UV pigments, LED rigs, and electric movement transform the performance space into a living circuit board. Every character glows; every appearance is engineered for blacklight and dusk-to-dark transitions.

Stiltwalking · Roaming

Lumira

the Glowwing — UV Butterfly

A creature stitched from blacklight and pollen-dust. Six-foot wings unfurl in electric pinks, greens, and ultraviolet whites. By day a riot of color; under UV she becomes pure radiant geometry, drawing the crowd like a flame draws moths.

Costume
UV-reactive bodysuit, six-foot pollen-dust wings, antenna headpiece, butterfly silhouette
Makeup
Geometric face paint that lights up under blacklight, neon pigment accents
Energy
Quick directional shifts, sudden stillness, head tilts like an insect catching a sound
Mood Board
Bioluminescent butterflies · Avatar's forest creatures · blacklight pollen · vintage fairy illustrations gone neon
Palette

Tone — Electric, playful, otherworldly

Aerial · Apparatus: LED Lyra

Faylux

the Neon Sprite — UV Fei

Neon pink and neon green pixie fairy — a Tinkerbell who got into the festival lights. Pointed ears, sharp pixie wings, hair streaked hot pink and lime. On the hoop she snaps and pops like a circuit firing.

Costume
UV-reactive bodysuit in electric pink and acid green color blocks, pointed pixie ears, sharp-cut neon pixie wings
Makeup
Geometric face paint in neon pinks and greens, blacklight-reactive accents
Apparatus
LED Lyra (programmable hoop)
Energy
Quick, sharp, mischievous. Drops snap, shapes pop. Playful, flirty, never still for long.
Mood Board
Tinkerbell on a rave bender · blacklight pixie art · neon pop-art · Y2K club kid fairies · fiber-optic figurines
Palette

Tone — Hyperkinetic, playful, electric

Aerial · Apparatus: Neon Green Hammock

Vexa

the Glow-Beast — UV Creature

Not human. Something older. Bone-and-sinew bodysuit painted in UV-reactive whites and acid greens. Movement is creature — angular, stalking, surprising. Audiences should be a little unsure what they're watching.

Costume
UV-reactive whites and acid greens painted bone-and-sinew bodysuit, alien headpiece, exaggerated silhouettes
Makeup
Skeletal/creature face paint in UV-reactive whites and acid greens, contorted blacklight features
Apparatus
Neon green hammock
Energy
Feral, stalking, surprising. Builds to high-impact aerial shapes that read like a creature climbing rather than performing.
Mood Board
Deep-sea creatures · Pan's Labyrinth · blacklight skeleton art · alien xenomorph silhouettes
Palette

Tone — Feral, otherworldly, unsettling-in-a-good-way

Flow Arts · Prop: 1–4 LED Hula Hoops

Nova

the Light-Threader — Holo Space Girl

A traveler from somewhere with better stars. Multi-hoop work — orbiting, isolating, trailing geometric light around her body like she's threading constellations.

Costume
Holographic iridescent bodysuit that shifts color as she moves, silver moon boots, futuristic visor or face crystals
Makeup
Cosmic glam — silver highlight, holographic gloss, space-age cheekbones
Prop
1–4 LED hula hoops (programmable patterns)
Energy
Cosmic, technical, mesmerizing. Geometric precision, hypnotic isolations.
Mood Board
Sailor Moon transformation sequences · holographic foil · planetariums · sci-fi anime · long-exposure star trails
Palette

Tone — Cosmic, technical, mesmerizing

Flow Arts · Prop: Open (Performer's Choice)

Opaline

the Prism-Veiled — Holographic Fae

An iridescent forest fae spun from soap-bubble light. Where Nova is cosmic and technical, Opaline is dreamy and floating — softer movement quality, fluttery transitions, an airier presence.

Costume
Holographic wings catching every angle of light, shimmering pastel-rainbow bodysuit that shifts color with every step
Makeup
Opal-toned base, crystal accents along cheekbones, soft pearlescent eye
Prop
Open — LED hoop, LED fans, LED poi, levitation wand, or silk veil depending on set time
Energy
Dreamy, floating, fluttery. Soft transitions, airy presence.
Mood Board
Soap bubbles · opal stones · dichroic film · fairy wings under window light · pastel iridescence
Palette

Tone — Dreamy, magical, soft-glow
