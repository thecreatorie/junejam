Day Two

Saturday
· Red ·

The forest catches fire.

Crimson·Ember·Smoke

Day two builds heat. The arc moves from earth (Mycelia, rooted and ancient) through danger (Vespera) to the burning peak (Ignix), settles into afterglow (Embriel), and ends in incense smoke (Saira). One fire, seen from five angles.

Stiltwalking · Roaming

Mycelia

the Cap of the Old Wood — Mega Mushroom

A towering fungal sovereign risen from the forest floor after the first summer rain. Her cap blooms wide and impossibly red, freckled with bone-white spots that seem to pulse in the dappled light.

Costume
Impossibly red mushroom cap freckled with bone-white spots, mossy stem with layered earth-tone fabric, vine and root details
Makeup
Bark-textured face paint with gold-leaf accents, woodland tones, earthy contour
Energy
Slow, certain, ceremonial. Patient, ancient, knowing. Bends like a tree to greet children.
Mood Board
Studio Ghibli forest spirits · Amanita muscaria mushrooms · Wendy Froud sculptures · Pan's Labyrinth fauns
Palette

Tone — Mythic, grounding, gently humorous

Aerial · Apparatus: Lyra or Silks

Vespera

the Crimson Heretic — Red Devil

She climbs the rig like she owns it. Slow, deliberate, dangerous. Less "scary devil," more fallen seraph who knows exactly what she's doing.

Costume
Blood-red latex or velvet bodysuit, horned headpiece, sharpened nails, slicked hair
Makeup
Smoky eyes, sharp red lip, dark contour, dramatic brow
Apparatus
Lyra or silks
Energy
Slow, deliberate, dangerous. Long inversions, suspended drops held a beat too long, eye contact that makes the crowd forget to breathe.
Mood Board
Fallen-angel iconography · Black Swan · vintage devil pin-up art · Mucha's darker work
Palette

Tone — Sultry, commanding, dangerous

Aerial · Apparatus: Lyra

Embriel

the Ash-Born — Red Butterfly Fae

A butterfly fae born from the last embers of a forest fire — rebirth in slow motion. Where Vespera is danger and Ignix is fire itself, Embriel is the warm afterglow.

Costume
Flowy red dress with movement-friendly drape — chiffon or silk that flows with every aerial rotation, regal headpiece, butterfly-inspired accents
Makeup
Warm metallic — copper highlight, deep crimson eye, gold-flecked liner
Apparatus
Lyra — the circular frame mirrors a butterfly's stillness on a flower
Energy
Slow inversions like a butterfly drying its wings, the dress trailing through every rotation, regal and unhurried.
Mood Board
Monarch butterflies · Mucha's autumnal figures · phoenix mythology · ember-glow photography · flamenco dress movement
Palette

Tone — Warm, regal, sensual, quietly mythic

Flow Arts · Prop: LED Fire Fans + LED Fire Mask

Ignix

the Phoenix-Touched — Simulated Fire

Phoenix energy without the burn permits. The illusion of fire dancing without any actual flame. Performs anywhere, any stage, any time.

Costume
Fire-themed bodysuit in oranges, reds, and golds with feathered or flame-cut details
Makeup
Glowing orange-and-red gradient under the LED fire mask, gold-leaf brow accents
Prop
LED fire fans + LED fire mask (programmed flame patterns, no open flame)
Energy
Fierce, mythic, untouchable. Programmed flame patterns ripple across the fans; the mask glows like coals.
Mood Board
Phoenix mythology · fire-eater performers · Hephaestus's forge · slow-motion fire footage
Palette

Tone — Fierce, mythic, untouchable

Flow Arts · Prop: Red Silk Fans

Saira

the Scarlet Sleeper — Red & Gold Odalisque

A red-and-gold odalisque pulled from incense smoke. Slow fan unfurls like opening peacock tails, undulations through the spine. Movement reads like incense smoke given form.

Costume
Silk harem pants, bejeweled crimson bra top, veiled face revealing only kohl-lined eyes, gold body chains, ankle bells
Makeup
Heavy kohl liner, deep red lip, henna-painted hands and feet, bindi accents
Prop
Red silk fans
Energy
Mysterious, sensual, exotic. Slow fan unfurls, spinal undulations.
Mood Board
Vintage orientalist paintings reimagined · Scheherazade · peacock feathers · incense in temple light
Palette

Tone — Mysterious, sensual, exotic

Caroline Godwin
