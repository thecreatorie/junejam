Each day, a new color world. Each character, a new spirit pulled from the forest.

The Concept

A Pantheon for the Forest

Friday opens the woods in electric neon. Saturday sets them ablaze in red. Sunday closes the ritual in shadow. Across three days, one performer transforms into a pantheon of woodland spirits — each distinct in silhouette, palette, and discipline.

Every day features stiltwalking (roaming character work), two aerial characters on a 16ft tripod rig, and two flow arts characters. Each character is a fully-realized look that can perform one or multiple sets throughout the day depending on programming needs.

Production

A Single Performer · A Full Cast
